AboutTom Sira

Tom Sira is a self-taught multi-instrumentalist and producer. His path began at the drums and led, by way of composition and jazz reharmonisation, into analog production. A deep understanding of harmony informs the work, yet it is reduction that defines it: ambient and tech house built on atmosphere, played mostly from vinyl.
